# Content and media rights

Original Hub summaries, descriptions, translations, indexes, and metadata remain under the copyright of their respective contributors unless a file states otherwise.

Third-party names, trademarks, source material, videos, screenshots, photographs, artwork, and other media remain the property of their respective owners. Inclusion of a source link or factual reference does not transfer or replace the original rights.

Every repository-hosted media asset must include machine-readable rights metadata with its creator or copyright holder, source URL, license identifier, license URL when available, attribution text, modification status, and whether the file may be offered for download by client applications.

YouTube videos are referenced by provider ID and played through the supported embedded player. Video files are not stored in this repository.

Full copies or translations of third-party articles, magazine pages, and transcripts are not accepted. Hub articles must use original wording, concise factual summaries, and clear source citations.
